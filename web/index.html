Interpretation and limitations

The 2026 Surviving Sepsis Campaign adult guidelines recommend immediate antimicrobials, ideally within one hour, for septic shock or probable/definite sepsis. For possible sepsis without shock, rapid assessment and antimicrobial therapy within three hours are conditionally suggested if concern for infection persists. A three-hour window is not a reason to postpone indicated treatment.

Other bundle tasks, including lactate reassessment, fluid resuscitation, and vasopressor support, depend on clinical indications; this worksheet does not judge their appropriateness or calculate formal compliance. A recorded time is not evidence that an intervention was clinically indicated or appropriate.

Reference: Surviving Sepsis Campaign, 2026. Follow local policy and the applicable measurement specification.
